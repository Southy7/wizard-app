    How this page works

    Help

      Special Cards

      Special cards used during a trick may be played even when you could follow suit.

      Shapeshifter

      Effect
      When played, choose whether it acts as a Fool or a Wizard. It then follows all rules of that card.
      Trump revealed
      The dealer chooses the trump suit.

      Dragon

      Effect
      The highest card, even above a Wizard. It wins every trick unless the Fairy is in the same trick.
      Leading a trick
      Use the same follow-suit rules as for a Wizard.
      Trump revealed
      The dealer chooses the trump suit.

      Fairy

      Effect
      The lowest card, below a Fool. If the Fairy and Dragon share a trick, the Fairy wins it.
      Leading a trick
      Use the same follow-suit rules as for a Fool.
      Trump revealed
      There is no trump suit this round.

      Bomb

      Effect
      Nobody wins the trick and it counts toward no bid. Whoever would have won leads the next trick.
      Leading a trick
      Use the same follow-suit rules as for a Fool.
      Trump revealed
      There is no trump suit this round.

      Werewolf

      Effect
      Before bidding, its holder swaps it for the revealed trump card, takes that card, and chooses any trump suit or no trump.
      Trump revealed
      The dealer chooses the trump suit.

      Juggler

      Effect
      Value 7½ in a chosen suit. After the trick, everyone passes one hand card face down clockwise, even with a Bomb, except after the final trick.
      Leading a trick
      The chosen suit must be followed.
      Trump revealed
      The dealer chooses the trump suit.

      Cloud

      Effect
      Value 9¾ in a chosen suit. The trick winner immediately changes their bid by −1 or +1. A Bomb in the same trick cancels this change.
      Leading a trick
      The chosen suit must be followed.
      Trump revealed
      The dealer chooses the trump suit.

      Witch

      Effect
      The lowest card, below the Fool and Fairy. After resolving the trick, swap one hand card for any card in it except the Witch. The new card has no effect.
      Leading a trick
      Use the same follow-suit rules as for a Fool.
      Trump revealed
      There is no trump suit this round.

      Vampire

      Effect
      Copies the revealed trump card and all its effects. If that card is the Werewolf, reveal another card and copy that instead.
      Leading a trick
      Use the follow-suit rules of the copied card.
      Trump revealed
      The dealer chooses the trump suit.

      The Dark Eye

      Effect
      Set it aside, draw three cards, choose one to play, and return the other two face down.
      Leading a trick
      The chosen card becomes the lead card and follows its normal rules.
      Trump revealed
      Draw three cards and choose one of them to determine the trump suit. Return the other two face down.
